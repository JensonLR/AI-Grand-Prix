# Delivery Roadmap

## 2027 browser release — complete

The agreed Connectome World Championship browser product checklist is complete and published through the validated `browser-preview` release path.

Completed scope includes:

1. Full 22-driver / 11-constructor championship product with a 24-round 2027 season, practice, qualifying, Sprint weekends, Grand Prix races and persistent standings.
2. Full pinned BANC v888 browser topology with 188,508 mapped nodes and 11,752,828 directed neuron pairs, independent driver neural state and a hard runtime-readiness gate.
3. Deterministic authoritative racing simulation with tyres, wear, wet grip, energy, fuel, damage, collisions, pit stops, Safety Car and red-flag handling.
4. Premium Three.js spectator experience with all 24 circuit layouts, multiple camera modes, timing tower, telemetry, circuit map, wet-weather presentation and race audio.
5. Deterministic AUTO TV direction for incidents, pit stops, close battles, final-lap action and race finish.
6. Animated pit-stop broadcast graphics and track-anchored 3D pit-lane crews with team-specific presentation.
7. Full post-race podium ceremony and classification presentation.
8. Eleven distinct spoof-constructor visual identities and livery/signature treatments on the premium AGP-27 car package.
9. Desktop/mobile layouts, touch Human Test controls, installable web-app metadata, low-power rendering path, vendor code splitting and hosted runtime caching.
10. Release gating through lint, typecheck, automated tests, 24/24 circuit drivability, 22/22 Super Licence certification, replay generation, production build, BANC verification and real Chromium render smoke before browser publication.

## Future ideas — new scope, not unfinished release work

The following are optional future experiments rather than outstanding requirements:

- a higher-fidelity rigid-body / suspension / combined-slip tyre model if a browser-safe implementation materially improves racing
- indexed binary/delta replay storage to reduce replay payload size
- a larger authored audio library
- additional cinematic/art variations beyond the completed constructor identity pass
- deeper long-session profiling on a wider device matrix

None of these are prerequisites for the completed and validated 2027 browser release.

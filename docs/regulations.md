# AI Grand Prix Sporting and Technical Regulations 0.2

## Championship structure

The Connectome World Championship contains exactly 22 persistent digital driver phenotypes and 11 constructors, with two championship drivers per constructor.

Drivers score toward the Drivers' World Championship. Both constructor cars score toward the Constructors' World Championship.

Default points are 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for P1-P10. The scoring table is data-driven.

## Driver identity

Championship driver phenotype seeds are permanent. Drivers may not be regenerated between practice, qualifying and races to chase a favourable result.

No manual `PACE`, `RAIN`, `CORNERING`, `AGGRESSION` or equivalent hidden performance ratings are permitted.

## Super Licence principle

An AGP Super Licence is a minimum competence threshold, not a ranking of the fastest generated phenotypes. Qualified championship drivers must demonstrate basic circuit following, braking, cornering, valid-lap completion, recovery and collision avoidance under a common curriculum.

## Constructor regulation

All constructors belong to one AGP-01 technical family and have the same total engineering opportunity. Static configuration differences are tightly bounded around the neutral car.

Permitted engineering dimensions include aero efficiency, downforce, mechanical grip, energy system, braking, tyre management, reliability and neural-interface/control response.

Constructor identity must be expressed through trade-offs rather than arbitrary global speed bonuses.

## Neural interface regulation

Every constructor receives the same category of sensory bandwidth, decoder complexity, latency and adaptation budget.

The motor decoder may receive neural/connectome activity only. It may not consume absolute track coordinates, an ideal racing line, target steering, target speed or an optimal brake point.

## Neutral Driver Test

The AGP-01N Neutral Driver Test uses the same neutral machine and conditions for every championship phenotype. Its analytical result may be used for comparisons but must not secretly change championship race performance.

## Race classification

Race order is derived from completed laps and circuit progress, with penalties applied by the sporting system when implemented. The simulation, not a benchmark table or script, decides the result.

## Conditions

Current technical support includes dry and wet grip, soft/medium/hard/intermediate/wet tyres, tyre thermal/wear state, energy state, contact and component damage.

Practice, qualifying, pit operations, yellow/VSC/safety-car/red-flag procedures and penalties must remain versioned as they mature. Unsupported rule behaviour must not be presented as complete.

## Scientific classification

A replay using the compact browser neural runtime is classified `NON-BENCHMARK` and labelled `AGP_SIMULATION_INTERFACE`.

A future BANC event may only claim `BANC_V888_IMPORT` after the source data, transformation pipeline, runtime and decoder boundary have been version-locked and validated.

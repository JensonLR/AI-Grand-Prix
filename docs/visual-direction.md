# Illustrated Realism

The physical world remains believable while colour and composition borrow the confidence of classic European travel posters: cobalt sky and sea, warm ivory light, ink shadows, vegetation green and restrained prestige gold. The live title scene uses a different circuit composition from the supplied reference.

No fake sponsors or generated copy are permitted. Visible track identity is limited to controlled AGP language. Cars use realistic open-wheel proportions and recognisable mechanical assemblies; production art must further refine suspension, floor, cockpit, aero surfaces, damage and materials.

## Scientific boundary

Do not claim 22 independently reconstructed biological flies. The championship uses 22 persistent **digital driver phenotypes** derived from one shared BANC graph architecture.

Do not claim biophysical completeness. BANC connectivity is source data; racing transduction, neural dynamics, phenotype perturbations, learning and the motor decoder are AI Grand Prix modelling assumptions.
